Where next, as a partner?

Distilled from thousands of hours spent working alongside partners — and the firms they lead.

You have made partner. The title, though, was never the destination: the best partners keep developing long after it is secured — commercially, as leaders of people, and as stewards of the firm's future. The discipline that got you here is the same discipline that keeps you at your best.

This diagnostic assesses you against the eight capability areas that define the partner role at its best. It takes about 20–25 minutes. Answer as you are today, not as you were at your best — the gaps are the point. Your results identify where your development and coaching attention will pay back most. Nothing is stored as you go; when you finish, you will be invited to send your results to Emperico and can print a copy for yourself.

Be honest rather than kind to yourself. Fellow partners, your team and your clients rarely see you quite the way you see yourself — so the most valuable use of this picture is to test it with someone who will tell you the truth.
